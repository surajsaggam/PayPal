## 1. Charlie Munger's Inversion Pre-Mortem ("Tell Me Where I'm Going to Die, So I Never Go There")

Before writing a single line of code, the War-Room Council—led by **Charlie Munger**, **Max Levchin**, and **Aaron J. Webster** (PayPal EVP & former Global Chief Risk Officer)—conducted an inversion pre-mortem asking: *"Assume it is December 2027 and PayOps AI has either been disqualified from the hackathon or shut down in production. What killed it?"*

Every failure mode identified in that pre-mortem is codified below into our **Canonical 12-Risk Register (`R-01` to `R-12`)**, matching `PROJECT.md § Interface Contracts` verbatim.
