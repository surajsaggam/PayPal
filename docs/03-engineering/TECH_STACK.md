## 1. Complete Technology Stack Decision Table (Andrej Karpathy & PayPal Stack Lens)

Every library, runtime, and cloud service in `PayOps AI` is pinned to a verified production version, chosen to maximize native synergy with **PayPal's engineering stack** (`@paypal/agent-toolkit`, PayPal MCP Server, `JunoDB`, `Cosmos.AI`, `Kraken.js`, `Fastlane`, `PYUSD`) and the **10 official Hackathon Sponsors**.
