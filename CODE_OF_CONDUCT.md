# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`)

## 1. Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **PayOps AI (`PayPal Sentinel`)** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy open-source financial technology community.

---

## 2. Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other builders, merchants, and users.
- Being respectful of differing technical opinions, viewpoints, and experiences: **attack ideas and architectures rigorously, never people**.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback during code reviews and architectural debates.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall safety, accessibility, and integrity of the global merchant and developer community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment in issues, pull requests, or community channels.
- Publishing others' private information (including merchant PII, sandbox/live credentials, or customer financial records) without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source setting.

---

## 3. Enforcement Responsibilities & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at `conduct@payops.ai`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, with strict confidentiality toward the reporter.
